# Science Intelligence™ Presentation — Territory-First Structure (default)

Reference build: the Kenvue Tylenol® SCC Claims deck (Oct 2026). Output is one markdown content brief plus CSVs; Claude Design styles and exports. 16:9, one idea per slide, tiles and cards on `#f0f0f0`.

## Flow

**Front matter (5 slides)**

1. **Cover** — "[Product]® Claims", SCIENCE INTELLIGENCE™ PRESENTATION lockup, prepared-for line, month/year.
2. **The Goal** — left: the business goal as one large statement (eyebrow THE GOAL). Right card: three numbered rows, `01 THE GAP`, `02 THE OPENING`, `03 OUR BRIEF`, 1–2 sentences each.
3. **Where Today Fits** — Science Marketing Engine™, three cards: Phase 01 Prove [TODAY] · Phase 02 Persuade [NEXT] · Phase 03 Produce [NOT INCLUDED, greyed]. Sub-chips under each (Lit Review™, Claim Dev™; Persuadable Research consumer testing; Studio formats).
4. **Where We Looked** — three big-number tiles (e.g., studies/sources mapped · systematic review · original analysis or official sources read in full), each with a 2–3 sentence explanation. Bottom strip: "RISK AREAS WE EXPLORED", 5–6 columns, bold label + one line.
5. **How Each Claim Territory Runs** — three cards with arrows: `01 THE CLAIM` (FOR THE BRAND TEAM) · `02 THE EVIDENCE` (FOR MLR AND THE BRAND) · `03 COMPLIANCE LOGIC` (FOR MLR), each with a one-line purpose and three bullets.

**Results**

6. **Divider** — "LIT REVIEW™ | CLAIM DEV™ | Results" + one sentence: how many territories, that each runs in three parts, and which (if any) is a future HCP campaign.

**Per territory (repeat; 4–6 territories)** — header on every slide: `TERRITORY N` box · territory name · sub-label (The Claim / The Evidence / Compliance Logic) · `LIT REVIEW™ | CLAIM DEV™` lockup top-right.

- **The Claim** — left: white hero card, eyebrow THE CLAIM, the hero claim in quotes (large), optional supporting line, then "WHY THIS CLAIM MATTERS TO [BRAND]" (2–3 sentences). Right: "OTHER WAYS TO SAY IT", 3–5 rows, each a pill tag + the line. Claims can name competitor brands only when the client allows it and the line quotes their label.
- **The Evidence** — left: hero data tile (eyebrow = source type, e.g. "NEJM 2019 · RANDOMIZED TRIAL"; giant number; one-line meaning; citation + "Full text verified" where true). Right: the visual that gets you to the number (table, chart, steps, label-by-label grid). Under it: "On the label." line or a regulator quote. Small footer: "Full study list in Appendix B."
- **(Optional) The Evidence · How we ran the numbers** — 4 step cards (STEP 01–04) for any original analysis or derived number; data files/fields listed; reproducibility line.
- **Compliance Logic** — eyebrow COMPLIANCE SUMMARY; 4–6 tiles in a 3×2 grid. Tile = short bold title + 1–2 sentences. Cover: where the number comes from · why the source holds up · framing (informs, doesn't attack; status not safety) · it's on their label / already approved · counter-evidence named · what's still open before MLR ("Needs a ruling", "Before MLR"). Close with risk + substantiation depth.

**HCP territory (optional, after consumer territories)** — one slide: "The Data · A potential future HCP campaign". Four numbered step tiles (title + stat + source), then a black "Why HCPs" bar.

**Legacy claims, strengthened** — rows: legacy approved claim (left, quoted) → strengthened line (bold) → territory pill.

**Close**

- **Divider** — Next Steps.
- **What happens next** — 7 step cards with owner tags (CLIENT / LET THERE BE / CLIENT MLR): feedback → revise territories → MLR territory check → build claims → MLR claim review → (claims testing if planned) → Science Story™.
- **What a Science Story looks like** — example concept built from one territory: headline, narrative, numbered claim cluster, highlighter legend; right side "What we measure" dials. Use placeholders for scores unless real data exists; label "illustrative format".
- **Who should we test with? / Calls we need from you** — 3 numbered cards.
- **Closing** — "Science leads. Creativity amplifies."

**Appendix**

- Index slide: A. per-territory study tables (workbook) · B. full study list · C. label capture · D. regulatory notes.
- **B. Sources** — 10 per slide: `# | STUDY ID (S-001) | CITATION` with PMID/DOI. Supply as `Appendix_B_sources.csv`.
- **C. Label capture** — `ID (L-001) | Product/source | Active or what was used | Marketer | captured date`.
- **D. Regulatory notes** — monograph/class identity, class-safe flag per element, "Before any consumer use" card; then required disclaimers per SKU and per claim type, plus the working-language footer.

## Copy rules for this format

- Hero claims and register variants may be punchier than deck copy; everything else follows the plain voice.
- Every tile is self-contained: title + 1–2 sentences. No paragraphs on territory slides.
- Every number on a slide carries its source ID and evidence basis (full text / abstract / label / official page).
- Counter-evidence appears on the evidence slide (small outlined card) and in Compliance Logic.
