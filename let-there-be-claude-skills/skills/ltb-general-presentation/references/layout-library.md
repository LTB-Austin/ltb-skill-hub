# Layout library — LTB Template — General Presentation

These are the template's 23 slides, by slide id (the file name in `project/slides/`). Choose layouts by the shape of the content. Lengths are guides that keep a slide from breaking. Headlines are always one line.

**Use sparingly:** the tile layouts (`three-cards`, `grid-four`). Austin finds boxed tiles everywhere "kinda meh." Before using one, try `two-column`, `bullets`, `process`, `compare` or `table`.

## Opening

| Slide | Use it for | Fields | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cover` | The first slide | Title (one line, ≤ 45 characters), subline (≤ 80), audience or date line | — |
| `agenda` | Decks of 10+ slides, or anything presented live to a client | 3–6 numbered items, ≤ 40 characters each | Short decks; the headlines already are the agenda |

## Narrative

| Slide | Use it for | Fields | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `divider` | Opening a new section in decks of 12+ slides | Section label, one-line title | Short decks |
| `statement` | One idea that should land on its own: the thesis, a turn in the story | One sentence, ≤ 120 characters | You have supporting points to show; use `text` |
| `text` | A point plus a short explanation | Headline, 1–2 short paragraphs (≤ 350 characters total) | The content is a list; use `bullets` |
| `bullets` | 3–5 parallel points | Headline, 3–5 bullets of ≤ 90 characters each | Points of very different weight; use `text` |
| `two-column` | Two related blocks, such as a problem and an answer, or what and why | Headline, two column labels, a short body in each | The columns are options to choose between; use `compare` |
| `split-image` | A point that needs a picture: a product, a frame, a screenshot | Headline, body (≤ 250 characters), one image | You have no real image; never use a placeholder picture in a final deck |

## Structure

| Slide | Use it for | Fields | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `three-cards` | Exactly three parallel items that each need a title and a line | Headline, 3 × (title ≤ 30, line ≤ 110) | Anything a list or two columns can carry |
| `grid-four` | Exactly four parallel items | Headline, 4 × (title ≤ 30, line ≤ 100) | Same as above |
| `compare` | Options or before/after, side by side | Headline, 2–3 options, each with a label and 3–4 rows | The items aren't alternatives |
| `process` | Steps in order, where order matters | Headline, 3–5 steps (title ≤ 25, line ≤ 90) | Dates matter more than order; use `timeline` |
| `timeline` | Dated phases or milestones | Headline, 3–6 dated rows (date, phase, one line) | — |

## Data

| Slide | Use it for | Fields | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stats` | 3–4 numbers that belong together | Headline, 3–4 × (number ≤ 7 characters, label ≤ 60), source line | The numbers aren't comparable |
| `big-stat` | The one number the slide exists for | The number (≤ 7 characters), one line of meaning (≤ 100), source | — |
| `chart` | A comparison or trend that's clearer drawn | Headline, bar data (≤ 8 bars), takeaway line, source | Two or three numbers; use `stats`. Put the key or legend inside the chart, never beside it |
| `table` | Exact values someone will scan or check | Headline, ≤ 6 columns × ≤ 8 rows | The point is a single comparison; use `chart` or `compare` |

## Visual

| Slide | Use it for | Fields | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `quote` | A client's or expert's words, verbatim | Quote (≤ 200 characters), name, title, organization | The quote isn't cleared for the audience |
| `image-full` | A full-bleed image: a set photo, a video frame | Image, optional caption (≤ 60) | You have no real image |
| `image-grid` | 3–4 images with short captions | Images, captions (≤ 30 each) | — |
| `team` | Who's on the work | 2–6 people: name, role, one line | External decks, unless the client asked who's on the team |

## Close

| Slide | Use it for | Fields | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `next-steps` | What happens after this meeting | 3–5 rows: step, owner, timing | — |
| `closing` | The last slide | The template's closing line and logo, as they are | — |

## Picking well
- Read the content's shape first: one idea → `statement`; two blocks → `two-column`; options → `compare`; order → `process`; dates → `timeline`; one number → `big-stat`; several numbers → `stats`; exact values → `table`.
- Vary the rhythm. No more than two slides in a row on the same layout.
- When nothing fits, use `text` and say so in the handback. Don't force content into a tile grid.
