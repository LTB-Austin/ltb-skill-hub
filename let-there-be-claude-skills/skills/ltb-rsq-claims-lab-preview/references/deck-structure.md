# Claims Lab Preview — the deck

The deck follows the look of the recent LTB previews (Cialis Together, PreserVision AREDS3) with the Science Story and Science Studio slides removed and two substantiation slides added.

**Screen count:** 14 with two territories (the default), 15 with three. The only variable-length part is the spotlight run (one slide per territory). Number screens sequentially; the labels below assume two territories.

Each screen is marked:

- **BOILERPLATE** — fixed copy. Reproduce as written. Only the date changes.
- **SEMI** — fixed frame and headings, variable content inside.
- **VARIABLE** — written for this product.

Every screen after the cover carries the footer: `Preview · directional · findings not validated · sources masked until the full Lit Review™`

Every screen has a **speaker note**: a talk track for a presenter who will walk the room through it live and may not have read the studies. Keep each to three to five sentences.

## 06–07 Spotlight studies — VARIABLE (one slide per territory)
Eyebrow: `SCIENCE INTELLIGENCE™ | LIT REVIEW™ | SPOTLIGHT STUDIES`
Heading: a plain statement of what the territory shows, e.g. "The hungriest tissue in your body" or "Female partner outcomes and preference."

Table, **four rows**. Columns: `Yr` · `Study` · `Key finding` · `Design`.
- **Yr** and **Study** (author et al. plus journal and identifier) are blurred.
- **Key finding**: one to three plain sentences with n and the result. Highlight the phrase that carries the point (Design renders it in the mint highlight). A finding that complicates the picture gets the purple highlight instead.
- **Design**: study type in a few words, plus anything a reviewer checks first: "Randomized, open-label, crossover"; "Placebo-controlled crossover, 12 patients"; "Primary human RPE cell culture."

Under the table, an italic **evidence note**, one or two sentences, naming the limitation of this set: open-label, self-report, dose above label, small n, cell or animal only, a population that doesn't match the buyer. End on what holds across the set and what doesn't. E.g. "Spontaneity and time worry separate from sildenafil in every trial we found. Confidence does not."

This note is mandatory on this deck. It is the line that tells the room we read the papers.

Speaker note: walk the four rows in one sentence each, then read the evidence note.

## Source masking

The room must see the science is real without being able to go read it. Blur **identifiers**, never substance. In Design: `filter: blur(4.5px); user-select: none` on the masked span only.

**Blurred:** publication years; author names; journal names; every PMID, DOI and NCT; author-year citations anywhere on a slide.

**Never blurred:** findings, n, designs, evidence notes, themes, claim lines, claim types, fit, risk, guardrails, and everything on boilerplate slides.

The presenter sheet carries every identifier in full, so questions can be answered in the room.

## Rules

- 14 screens with two territories, 15 with three. Four studies per spotlight. Three or four claims. One line-sits claim.
- No Science Story, Science Studio, worked example, test scores or prices.
- Every spotlight slide carries an evidence note. Every claim appears on slide 11.
