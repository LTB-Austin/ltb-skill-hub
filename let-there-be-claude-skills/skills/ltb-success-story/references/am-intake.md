# Account manager intake

Search the project files first. Then send this as one message to the account manager, or give it to the user to forward. Pre-fill every answer you found in the files and mark it **(from files, please confirm)**, so the AM only confirms it or corrects it. Ask only for what's missing. Keep it short, because the AM is busy.

---

**Subject: Success Story — [Brand]: a few things I need from you**

I'm building the [Brand] Success Story. Most of it comes from the project files, but these answers have to come from you. Short answers are fine.

**The brief**
1. In the client's words, what did they ask us for? *(pre-fill from SOW / kickoff, please confirm)*
2. Who was the client team? Names and titles of anyone we might quote or thank, plus the parent company name for the closing logo.

**Dates**
3. When did the engagement start and end?
4. What were the key approval dates: claims approved, story approved, final assets approved?
5. If there was a shoot, where and when was it?

**Where it is now**
6. Where is the work live today: channels, retailers, placements?
7. Is anyone else rolling it out (agency, broker, retail partner)? Can we name them?
8. Has the client shared any results (sales, conversion, ROAS, rankings, retailer wins)? If yes, give the figures, the timeframe, and confirmation that we can show them. If not, that's fine. We won't estimate any.

**In their words**
9. Do we have any client quotes about the work: from the closing call, email or a survey? Please send the exact wording, with name and title.
10. Are those quotes cleared for use in a deck we show other prospects?
11. Was a feedback survey run? If yes, what were the answers for Quality, Communication, Expertise, Value, Timeliness and "Work again"?

**Images**
12. Please send four photos (shoot, set, team, closing call) and a short caption for each.
13. Please send the approved hero packshot, and the parent-company logo.

Anything you can't answer, tell me and I'll leave it out.

---

## Handling answers

- Put each answer in the source log, tagged "AM, [date]".
- If a quote hasn't been cleared, leave it out. You can list it in open items as "awaiting clearance."
- Results with no timeframe or no clearance don't go in.
- If the AM says a partner can't be named, describe their role instead ("through the brand's retail broker").
