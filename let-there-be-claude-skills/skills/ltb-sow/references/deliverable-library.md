# LTB deliverable library

This is description language for deliverables that **are in the pricing input**. Don't add anything from it that the input doesn't list. Customize the brand, product, mechanism and channel references, but keep the structural meaning. When the input gives specs, they override the typical specs listed here.

The engine has three phases. Present deliverables under the phase they belong to.

## Science Intelligence™

### Lit Review™
A systematic search and full-text screening of the published literature. It establishes what the evidence supports for the product and what the brand can say about it.

> Systematic search and full-text screening across [literature areas, e.g. eyelid hygiene, dry eye, cosmetics and preservative literature]. [Client]'s studies also evaluated.

- If a preview was shared, say that the Lit Review is the systematic version of that pass and starts from the territories the preview surfaced.
- Note any formula-match work, such as matching the US formula against the product used in the studies.
- Optional: sample territory explorations (3–4 tiles, each a title plus one preview data point) with the preview disclaimer.

### Claim Dev™
Turns the evidence into a documented, approvable claims library, then supports it through the client's MLR.

Bullets (use the ones that apply):

- Claims documented to [regulatory standard: cosmetic / DSHEA structure-function / OTC monograph] standards, positioned as [category].
- Separate HCP claims where the evidence supports more.
- Substantiation per claim, prepared for [Client]'s MLR review.
- Approved claims list for the marketing content to build on.

## Science Story™

### Science Story™ directions
> [Two to three] story directions built from the approved claims. Each [does the job this brand needs, e.g. explains why eye care starts at the lid], in consumer language. Different hooks, narratives, and claims are tested.

### Consumer testing (only if in scope)
> [200–300] consumers in the [category] category. Each direction scored as a whole, then claim by claim, on purchase intent, uniqueness and believability. Output: a validated consumer story.

- Name the testing partner only if the input does (e.g. Persuadable Research).
- Metric chips: Purchase intent · Uniqueness · Believability · Word-level heatmap.
- Speaker notes: "We know which words do the work before scripting."

## Science Studio™

### Ad-to-Education Sequence™
This is a two-video micro-funnel. A short Video Ad brings in awareness and click-through. A Consumer Education Video on the product page then delivers the Science Story™. It's presented as one screen with two linked cards.

**Video Ad card:** `VIDEO AD · :15 · 16:9 AND 9:16` / headline "Awareness, built for clicks"
> Runs as Amazon Sponsored Brand Video. Delivers awareness messaging to relevant consumers. Click-throughs to the product page.
> Animation, [stock footage / live-action application] (TBD), on-screen text, VO and music. Clear with or without sound, mobile-first, built to Amazon SBV guidelines.

**Consumer Education Video card:** `CONSUMER EDUCATION VIDEO · :30 · 16:9` / headline "The Science Story, on the PDP"
> Placed on the Amazon product page, Storefront, brand site and social. Educates on [mechanism / formula / correct use].
> Animation, [stock footage / live-action application] (TBD), on-screen text, VO and music.

**Footer:** "In sequence: the paid ad brings the consumer to the page; the video explains the product before they scroll."

**Speaker notes (standard):** The Video Ad and Consumer Education Video work together in sequence. The Video Ad runs as Amazon Sponsored Brand Video, delivering awareness messaging to relevant consumers and encouraging click-through to the product page. Once on the product page, the Consumer Education Video delivers the Science Story, educating on [mechanism]. In sequence, this provides awareness then education, raising product page visits and sales.

### Science Story Carousel
> Amazon gives a product page five to six image slots. Most brands fill them with disconnected graphics. The carousel turns the tested Science Story™ into a sequence a shopper can swipe through silently and come away knowing what [product] is, why [the core idea], and what makes [the product] different.

- Show the illustrative sequence as 5–6 numbered frames, one title each. The sequence runs: product + one-line story → why it matters → the problem made visible → how the product works → the approved clinical claim → how to use, plus a brand-credibility line.
- Footer: "Illustrative sequence. Final slide order and copy follow the tested story and MLR review. Delivered to Amazon image specs[, with new product imagery for the current pack]."
- Same art direction as the videos, so the page reads as one story.

### MOA Moment™
> An MOA Moment™ is a short 5–10 second science visualization, designed to communicate a complex concept fast. [Describe the mechanism to be visualized.] Integrate it into content across the ecosystem: PDP images, inside videos, brand site, social.

- Typical specs: 16:9, 9:16 and/or 1:1. Confirm them from the input. Slot: `[Example MOA Moment visuals — insert manually]`.

### Influencer Science Kit™
> Influencers are great at recommending products from personal experience but often struggle to explain scientific reasons to believe. Our Influencer Science Kit™ gives creators tools and guides to integrate simple science messaging and visuals into their content.

- What's inside: Simplified Science Story · One-liners and copy ideas · MOA Moment visuals.
- Always include "LTB does not manage influencers directly."

### Paid traffic ads (Studio programs)
Product-specific consumer video ads in lengths such as :15, :30 and :60. They open on a strong hook and link clearly to the product. Give one table row per length, listing audience, dimensions and additional uses (social, digital, paid placements, website, HCP sharing).

### Education video (Studio programs)
A longer full-story video, such as :120 at 16:9, that carries the whole mechanism arc. It's often not focused on one product and covers the product suite. Typical uses: internal training, HCP education, consumer education.

### Storyline (Studio programs)
A six-beat mechanism-of-action arc per program, written so the creative team and the client can see the whole story before scripting. The long-form video carries the full arc. Shorter assets distill it around the featured product.
