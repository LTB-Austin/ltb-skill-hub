# SOW deck structure

Every screen has an **eyebrow** (small caps label), a **headline** (one line), body fields, and **speaker notes**. Screens are numbered in the footer by the template. Sub-brand marks go only on the screen about that phase or deliverable.

---

## Shape A — Engine program (reference: iVIZIA Wipes, 18 screens)

| # | Screen | Eyebrow | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Title | STATEMENT OF WORK | Product name (with ®/™) · program label (e.g. E-COMMERCE PROGRAM) · PREPARED FOR [COMPANY] · [MONTH YEAR]. Science Marketing Engine™ mark. `[Insert packshot]` |
| 02 | The Challenge | THE CHALLENGE | Headline: the consumer misunderstanding in one line ("Consumers file the wipe under makeup removal."). 2–3 short paragraphs: why the misunderstanding happens; what it costs commercially (conversion → ROAS → spend; retail). Closing line on its own: the plain challenge statement ("The wipe has to be understood as a step in eye care before it can be sold as one."). |
| 03 | The Bigger Picture *(optional)* | THE BIGGER PICTURE | Headline: the strategic logic in one line ("Amazon proves consumer demand. Retail follows."). Three numbered columns, each `0n · LABEL` / title / 1–2 sentences: what the client leads · what this program does · the outcome. One-line close tying them. |
| 04 | The Engine | OUR APPROACH | Three columns: `01 · THE PROOF` Science Intelligence™ · `02 · THE PERSUASION` Science Story™ · `03 · THE PRODUCTION` Science Studio™. One sentence each, specific to this brand and scope. Drop a column if that phase isn't in scope. |
| 05 | § Science Intelligence | PHASE 01 · THE PROOF | Divider. Headline: what the phase settles ("What the evidence supports. What iVIZIA Wipes can say."). Science Intelligence™ mark. |
| 06 | Lit Review & Claim Dev | SCIENCE INTELLIGENCE™ · DELIVERABLES | Headline ("The systematic review, then the claims."). Lit Review™ block: the search scope in one or two sentences, naming the literature areas and the client's own studies. *Optional:* SAMPLE TERRITORY EXPLORATIONS, 3–4 tiles, each a territory title plus one data point, with the preview disclaimer line. Claim Dev™ block: 3–4 bullets (standard, regulatory class, any HCP tier, substantiation per claim, approved claims list). Lit Review™ and Claim Dev™ marks. |
| 07 | § Science Story | PHASE 02 · THE PERSUASION | Divider. Headline ("Turning the literature and claims into storylines."). Science Story™ mark. |
| 08 | Science Story & Testing | SCIENCE STORY™ · DELIVERABLES | Headline (what the story does for this audience). Two blocks: SCIENCE STORY™ DIRECTIONS (how many, built from approved claims, what each must do) · CONSUMER TESTING · [PARTNER] (sample size, category, what's scored, the output). Metric chips: Purchase intent · Uniqueness · Believability · Word-level heatmap. |
| 09 | § Science Studio | PHASE 03 · THE PRODUCTION | Divider. Headline ("[n] assets, all built for [channel]."). Science Studio™ mark. |
| 10+ | Studio deliverables | SCIENCE STUDIO™ · DELIVERABLE 0n | One screen per deliverable, or per sequence. The Ad-to-Education Sequence™ is one screen with two linked cards (Video Ad → Consumer Education Video): length, dimensions, purpose headline, placement, production method, and a one-line "In sequence…" footer. See `deliverable-library.md`. |
| n | Above the Fold *(when 2+ Studio assets work on one page)* | HOW THE ASSETS WORK TOGETHER | Headline ("The whole story, above the fold on a phone."). 3–4 bullets in shopper order: ad brings them → carousel tells it silently → video tells it with sound → shopper understands before the bullets/A+/reviews. Phone mock with labels (Education Video · CAROUSEL · [n] IMAGES + VIDEO · ADD TO CART). |
| n | Timeline | TENTATIVE TIMELINE | Headline: the duration ("Five months from kickoff to live on Amazon."). One row per phase: START DATE · phase name · 1–2 lines of activities, including where MLR sits. Footer: "Tentative. We align on final dates at kickoff; [Client]'s review rounds and MLR timing determine the calendar." |
| n | How We Work | HOW WE WORK | Condensed three-column form from `boilerplate.md`: Milestone approach · Copyright & usage · Retailer rejections (drop the third column if no retailer). |
| n | Working Together | HOW WE LIKE TO WORK WITH CLIENTS | Verbatim from `boilerplate.md`, Engine variant. |
| n | Investment | INVESTMENT | Engine layout (below). |
| n | Next Steps | NEXT STEPS | Numbered, 3–4 items (see `boilerplate.md`). |
| n | Closing | — | Verbatim closing. Cell imagery. |

**Single-phase (Shape C):** Title → Challenge → (Bigger Picture) → The Engine (show all three phases, mark the in-scope one, one line on what follows later) → the phase's divider and deliverable screen(s) → Timeline → How We Work → Working Together → Investment → Next Steps → Closing.

## Investment layouts

All figures come from the pricing input. Never compute a discount or total that the input doesn't support. If you do the arithmetic, check it, and show only lines the input names.

**Engine layout (iVIZIA):** the left column lists the scope by phase:

```
Science Marketing Engine™
Science Intelligence™   • Lit Review™  • Claim Dev™ and MLR support
Science Story™          • 2–3 story directions  • Consumer testing, 200–300 shoppers
Science Studio™         • Video Ad, :15  • Consumer Education Video, :30  • Science Story Carousel
```

The right column is a ledger:

```
Program investment · all-inclusive        $140,000
[n]% discount                             –$35,000      (only if in input)
Discounted program investment             $105,000      (only if discounted)
Contingency · held on the PO, not intended to be invoiced    $10,000
Total PO amount                           $115,000
```

The footer gives the invoicing or payment note from the input (e.g. "The project is fully invoiced within 2026.").

**Studio layout (Thorne):** a ledger:

```
Project fee           $120,000   Let There Be fees for all deliverables in this SOW.
Contingency           $10,000    Held on the PO for scope increases or backtracking. We do not intend to invoice it.
Total PO request      $130,000   We target invoicing $120,000.
```

Then add the deliverables summary (assets and files by program) and the payment terms from the input (e.g. 50% upon signing · 30% upon storyboard approval · 20% upon final delivery).

**Line-item layout** (when the input prices each deliverable separately): use a table of Deliverable · Details (or Duration · Dimensions) · Investment, one row per line item, then Contingency, then **Total PO amount**.

## Speaker notes

Every screen gets notes. They are the presenter's script in plain sentences:

- **Challenge:** the reasoning chain behind the problem, for example "OcuSoft survives on shelf because it has had thirty years to explain itself."
- **Deliverables:** what each one is, and why it's in this scope.
- **Timeline:** the dates as tentative, and what decides them.
- **Investment:** the figures read out in words, with the contingency explained.
- **Divider screens:** one sentence on what the phase does.
