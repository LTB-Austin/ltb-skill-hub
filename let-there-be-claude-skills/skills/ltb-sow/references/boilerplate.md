## Milestone Approach

> Unlike agencies that limit revisions, Let There Be uses a milestones approach to deliver the strongest possible deliverables.
>
> We offer unlimited revisions within each phase, aligned to the agreed timeline. For example, [scripts / claims / storyboards] can be refined as many times as needed within the allotted window. Once a phase is approved or its timeline concludes, we move forward into the next stage.
>
> If needed, phase timelines can be extended to allow for additional iteration, with the understanding this may impact overall delivery timing.
>
> Changes to approved work or backtracking between phases may require additional time and cost.

## Retailer Rejection Contingencies (only when Amazon or another online retailer is in scope)

> Sometimes online retailers like Amazon reject content that should meet their specs and standards. Should this happen, it is included in this scope to make revisions related to specific retailer rejections until the videos are approved and live on retailer sites.

## Working Together

Eyebrow: HOW WE LIKE TO WORK WITH CLIENTS · Headline: "Three agreements that keep projects moving."

**Inactivity**
- If a project goes quiet for a full month, two options...
- $3,000 per month of inactivity keeps our team ready next-day.
- Or formally pause: we invoice work to date and take a few weeks to ramp back up when you restart

**MLR involvement**
- Engine variant:
  - Claim development succeeds more often when we join conversations with reviewers
  - We explain the studies we found and the compliance logic behind each claim
  - Fewer rounds, faster approvals
- Studio variant:
  - Scripts and storyboards clear review more often when we join conversations with reviewers
  - We explain the science and the compliance logic behind each claim
  - Fewer rounds, faster approvals

**$10,000 buffer**
- Added to the PO; we do not intend to invoice it
- Lets us act on special requests or backtracking without redoing PO paperwork
- We talk before tapping it. Goal: we finish $10,000 under budget
